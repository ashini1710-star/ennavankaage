💌 A Letter From Me

En Kanney 🤍,

I don't really know where to begin, because there are so many things I want to tell you and somehow words never feel enough.

When I first knew you existed, you were just that tall boy from my primary-school days and the district kabaddi competitions paalkova skin, nonchalant face, Angry Bird expression, and apparently no interest in smiling at strangers. 😂 I was the girl who smiled at everyone, so I never imagined that one day you would become the person behind so many of my smiles.

Then life brought us into United Malaysia Hindu Voice. Slowly, work made us talk more. Your random check-ups during Culinary for Survival became daily conversations. You would ask about my day, listen to my yapping, and somehow I began depending on those conversations more than I realised.

At a time when I felt helpless with family worries, the weight of Year 2 medicine and my First Professional ahead of me, and hurts from people I trusted you arrived like something I didn't know I needed. I could tell you things in detail that even people close to me didn't know. You became the person who could make the noise in my head quiet down.

You were, in the simplest words, a கேளாமலே கையில் வந்த கட்டித் தங்கம். 🤍

And then came that one Sunday when I was leaving for university and I couldn't bear the thought of going without seeing you. I asked you, “Virocchan, what are we actually?”

And your answer became one of the sweetest moments of my life:

“I love you Subashini… will you eat in my echil banana leaf?” 😭❤️

Who needs a grand proposal when the person you love can make something so effortless feel like the most precious thing in the world?

You were my best friend first. And somehow, my best friend became my love.

So this little website is my way of giving you something from far away. I couldn't show up with food or a gift from here, but I can give you my words, my memories, my silly thoughts and all the love I carry for you.

Thank you for finding me. Thank you for staying. Thank you for choosing me.

Love, always,
Your Subashini 🤍🌷
